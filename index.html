Lynda Chehami, HDR — Associate Professor

Université Polytechnique Hauts-de-France INSA Hauts-de-France IEMN – site de Valenciennes, CNRS UMR 8520 CNRS
Welcome to my academic website.

My research in physical acoustics focuses on ultrasonics, guided waves, passive imaging and ambient-noise interferometry, with applications to structural health monitoring and the characterization of complex materials and structures.

My work combines wave physics, signal processing and experimental ultrasonics, with particular interest in thin and multilayer structures.

I currently serve as Chair of the Ultrasounds Technical Committee of the European Acoustics Association (EAA).
