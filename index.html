Lynda Chehami, HDR – Associate Professor in Physical Acoustics

Université Polytechnique Hauts-de-France INSA Hauts-de-France IEMN – site de Valenciennes, CNRS UMR 8520 CNRS
Welcome to my academic website to learn more about my research, teaching and scientific activities.

I am Lynda Chehami, HDR Associate Professor in Physical Acoustics at Université Polytechnique Hauts-de-France (UPHF), INSA Hauts-de-France, and the IEMN CNRS UMR 8520 laboratory in Valenciennes, France.

My research focuses on ultrasonics, ultrasonic guided waves, passive imaging, ambient-noise interferometry, wave correlation, coda-wave analysis, structural health monitoring, thin films, multilayer structures, and advanced materials characterization.

My work combines wave physics, signal processing and experimental ultrasonics for the characterization and monitoring of complex structures and materials.

I currently serve as Chair of the Ultrasounds Technical Committee of the European Acoustics Association (EAA).
