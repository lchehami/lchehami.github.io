I was pleased to contribute to **Forum Acusticum 2026**, held in Graz, Austria, through the proposal and organisation of scientific sessions, as well as the presentation of our latest research.

As **Chair of the EAA Technical Committee on Ultrasounds**, I actively contributed to the scientific programme, fostering international exchanges and collaborations within the acoustics community.
