    <strong>Theme Chair</strong> for
    <strong>Acoustics 2027</strong>,
    the joint international meeting of the
    Acoustical Society of America (ASA)
    and the European Acoustics Association (EAA).
  </p>

  <h3>Scientific Programme and Responsibilities</h3>

  <p>
    As Theme Chair, I am contributing to
    the development and coordination of
    the scientific programme, including
    thematic sessions and international
    scientific exchanges.
  </p>

  <p>
    The conference will bring together
    researchers, academics, students and
    industry representatives from across
    the international acoustics community.
  </p>

  <p>
    I look forward to fostering scientific
    discussions, promoting emerging research
    directions and strengthening international
    collaborations in acoustics and ultrasonics.
  </p>
