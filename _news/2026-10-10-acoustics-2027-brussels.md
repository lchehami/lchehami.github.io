## Acoustics 2027 – Brussels, Belgium

I am pleased to serve as **Theme Chair** for **Acoustics 2027**, the joint meeting of the Acoustical Society of America (ASA) and the European Acoustics Association (EAA), taking place in Brussels, Belgium, from **21 to 25 June 2027**.

I am contributing to the development and coordination of the scientific programme, including the organisation of thematic sessions.
