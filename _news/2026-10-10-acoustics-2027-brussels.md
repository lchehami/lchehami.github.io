I am pleased to serve as **Theme Chair** for **Acoustics 2027**, the joint international conference of the Acoustical Society of America (ASA) and the European Acoustics Association (EAA), taking place in **Brussels, Belgium, from 21 to 25 June 2027**.

In this role, I contribute to the development and coordination of the scientific programme, including the organisation of thematic sessions.
