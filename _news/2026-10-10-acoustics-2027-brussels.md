  <h2>Acoustics 2027 – Brussels, Belgium</h2>
  <p><strong>21–25 June 2027</strong></p>

  <p>
    I am pleased to serve as
    <strong>Theme Chair</strong> for
    <strong>Acoustics 2027</strong>,
    the joint international meeting of the
    Acoustical Society of America (ASA) and
    the European Acoustics Association (EAA).
  </p>

  <p>
    In this role, I am contributing to the
    development and coordination of the
    scientific programme, including thematic
    sessions and international scientific
    exchanges.
  </p>

  <h3>Scientific Programme</h3>
  <p>
    The conference will bring together
    researchers, academics and industry
    representatives from the international
    acoustics community.
  </p>
