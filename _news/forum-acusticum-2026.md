## Forum Acusticum 2026 – Graz, Austria

I was pleased to contribute to the scientific programme of **Forum Acusticum 2026** through the proposal and organisation of scientific sessions.

As **Chair of the EAA Technical Committee on Ultrasounds**, I contributed to the scientific exchanges within the European acoustics community.

### Scientific activities

- **Session organisation:** [Physical Acoustics and Ultrasound – A13.00](https://indico.iem.at/event/10/sessions/687/)
- **Scientific presentation:** *Detection and Scattering Response Estimation of a Heterogeneity in a Thin Elastic Plate using Lamb Wave Beamforming and Reverberation Analysis.*
