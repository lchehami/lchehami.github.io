  <h2>Forum Acusticum 2026 – Graz, Austria</h2>
  <p><em>September 2026</em></p>

  <p>
    I was pleased to participate in
    <strong>Forum Acusticum 2026</strong>,
    held in Graz, Austria, bringing together
    researchers and experts from across the
    international acoustics community.
  </p>

  <p>
    As <strong>Chair of the European Acoustics
    Association (EAA) Technical Committee on
    Ultrasounds</strong>, I contributed to the
    scientific programme through the proposal
    and organisation of thematic sessions,
    promoting scientific exchanges and
    international collaborations.
  </p>

  <p>

    discussions on recent developments in
    physical acoustics, ultrasound,
    guided elastic waves, non-destructive
    testing (NDT), structural health

  <h3>Oral Scientific Presentation</h3>

  <p>
    I also presented our research entitled:
  </p>

  <p>
    <strong>
      Detection and Scattering Response
      Estimation of a Heterogeneity in a
      Thin Elastic Plate using Lamb Wave
      Beamforming and Reverberation Analysis
    </strong>
  </p>

  <p>
    This work investigates the detection
    and characterisation of heterogeneities
    in thin elastic plates using guided
    ultrasonic waves.
  </p>

  <p>
    The approach combines
    <strong>Lamb wave beamforming</strong>
    with the analysis of
    <strong>reverberant elastic wave fields</strong>
    to study the scattering response
    associated with local heterogeneities.
    These developments contribute to
    ultrasonic imaging methods for
    non-destructive evaluation and
    structural health monitoring.
  </p>
