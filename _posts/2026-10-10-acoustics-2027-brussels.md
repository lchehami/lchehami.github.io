  <h2>Acoustics 2027 – Brussels, Belgium</h2>
  <p><em>October 2026</em></p>

  <p>
    I am pleased to serve as
    <strong>Theme Chair</strong> for
    <strong>Acoustics 2027</strong>,
    the joint meeting of the
    Acoustical Society of America (ASA)
    and the European Acoustics Association (EAA).
  </p>

  <p>

    <strong>Brussels, Belgium,
    from 21 to 25 June 2027</strong>.
  </p>

  <p>
    In this role, I am contributing to the
    development and coordination of the
    scientific programme, including the
    organisation of thematic sessions.
  </p>

  <p>
    I look forward to fostering international
    collaborations and scientific exchanges
    within the acoustics and ultrasound
    communities.
